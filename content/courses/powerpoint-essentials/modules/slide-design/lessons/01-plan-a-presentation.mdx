# Plan a Clear Presentation

Before opening a slide editor, write one sentence describing what the audience should understand or do after the presentation. That purpose helps decide what belongs in the deck.

Give each slide one main job. A useful sequence might introduce a question, show the evidence, explain what it means, then end with a conclusion or next step. The sequence should fit the subject rather than follow a fixed template.

<Callout type="info">
  A slide is a visual aid for a speaker or a reader. If a slide needs a paragraph of small text to
  make sense, split the idea or provide the detail in a companion document.
</Callout>

## Review the slide before presenting

- Use a title that states the slide's point.
- Keep text large enough to read from the back of the room.
- Check text and chart labels against their background for strong contrast.
- Add alternative text to meaningful images and describe important visual details aloud.
- Remove any element that does not support the slide's point.
